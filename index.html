Biography

I have pursued a dual path of academic and Islamic scholarship earning an MA in Urdu Literature from Shibli National College, and continuing my religious training at Al-Mustafa University in Qom, Iran, where I completed an MA in the Sciences of the Qur'an and undertook advanced studies in Bahse Kharij. My areas of specialization span Qur'anic interpretation and sciences, philosophy and theology, mysticism, and ethics.

For over fifteen years I have served communities across India, East Africa, Madagascar, and Réunion (France) as a resident scholar, Imam of Jumu'ah and Jama'at, teacher, and principal.
